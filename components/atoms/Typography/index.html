Body Text

This is lead text used for introductory paragraphs. It's larger and lighter to draw attention without overwhelming the reader.

Body XL text is used for important content that needs more emphasis. Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit.

Body Large text is perfect for longer content sections where readability is important. This size is comfortable for extended reading.

Body Base is the standard text size used throughout the application. It provides optimal readability for most content types and is the most commonly used text style.

Body Small is used for secondary information, captions, and supporting text. It's still readable but takes up less visual space.

Body XS is the smallest body text size, used for fine print, timestamps, and minimal supporting information.
